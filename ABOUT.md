# About the Project — India District Data Portal

## Overview

India District Data Portal brings district-level survey data together in one place. It helps users explore health and nutrition indicators, find district information, and compare available figures.

## What the Portal Offers

- **Explore districts:** Select a state and browse its districts on the interactive map or by name.
- **Search indicators:** Look up specific indicators across health, nutrition, and demographics.
- **Compare data:** Compare district values with state-level figures wherever data is available.
- **View district details:** Examine charts, survey figures, and demographic profiles for any district.

## How to Use the Portal

1. **Choose a state and district:** Select a state from the map explorer or use the search box to find a district.
2. **Select an indicator:** Pick an indicator you want to explore across health, nutrition, or education.
3. **Review and compare:** Review the available figures and compare them with state data where possible.

## Data Sources

The portal uses data from the fifth round of the National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5), conducted between 2019 and 2021 by the International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS) under the stewardship of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India.

Indicator values and comparisons shown on this website are limited to the figures available in this survey dataset. They reflect the 2019–21 survey period and should not be treated as live administrative statistics or official rankings.
